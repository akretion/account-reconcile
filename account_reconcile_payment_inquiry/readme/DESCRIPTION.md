This module allows the accounting team to ask the Sales Administration to
identify a bank transaction which cannot be reconciled with an invoice.

In the bank reconciliation widget, a new button **Ask for Identification**
(*Contacter ADV* in French) is available next to the *To Check* button: it
flags the transaction and records which user asked for the identification.
While the Sales Administration has not answered, the flagged transaction
displays an information banner in the bank reconciliation view and the
accountant can click on **Ask for Identification Again** (*Relancer l'ADV* in
French) to follow up on the request. The transactions waiting for an answer
can also be hidden from the bank reconciliation view with the filter **Hide
Payment Inquiries To Answer** of its search bar.

The flagged transactions are available in a dedicated menu **Payment
Inquiries**, in the *Orders* menu of the Sales app, for the users of the new
**Payment Inquiry** group (a technical group): they are notified by email when
an identification is asked (and when it is asked again), they write what the
payment corresponds to and, when they answer, the user who asked for the
identification receives the answer in a planned activity on the journal entry
of the transaction.
