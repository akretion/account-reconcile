This module allows the accounting team to ask the Sales Administration
to identify a bank transaction which cannot be reconciled with an
invoice.

In the bank reconciliation widget, a new button **Ask for
Identification** (*Contacter ADV* in French) is available next to the
*To Check* button: it flags the transaction and records which user asked
for the identification. The flagged transactions are hidden by default
in the bank reconciliation view: the default filter **Hide Payment
Inquiries To Answer** of its search bar can be unchecked by the
accountant to reconcile a transaction before the answer of the Sales
Administration.

The flagged transactions are available in a dedicated menu **Payment
Inquiries**, in the *Orders* menu of the Sales app, for the users of the
new **Payment Inquiry** group (a technical group): they write what the
payment corresponds to and, when they answer, the user who asked for the
identification receives the answer in a planned activity on the journal
entry of the transaction.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

- give the technical **Payment Inquiry** group to the Sales
  Administration users,
- in the bank reconciliation widget, click on **Ask for Identification**
  on a transaction which cannot be reconciled: the transaction is
  flagged, it records which user asked for the identification and it is
  hidden from the bank reconciliation view,
- the flagged transaction is available in the **Payment Inquiries** menu
  (Sales > Orders) of the Sales Administration users, where they write
  what the payment corresponds to and click on **Answer and Notify**,
- the user who asked for the identification receives the answer in a
  planned activity (To-Do type) on the journal entry of the transaction
  and the transaction is available again in the bank reconciliation
  view.

The users of the *Payment Inquiry* group can only modify the payment
inquiry fields on the bank transactions: they cannot modify the other
information of the transactions.
